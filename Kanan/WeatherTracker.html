Erinn Weather

Weather changes every 20 minutes, at the times shown, fading in over the first minute. It comes straight from your game, so it's what your game will show.

Where you are: keep this page open while you play and it follows you. The line under the title says where you are, and that place's card and timeline row are marked "You're here". They update within a few seconds of changing regions, while the game is running.

Refresh: type .weather in game (or use Weather Tracker in the Kanan menu) for a fresh page. Do it after restarting the game, since the slots marked "may differ" change with each game session; after a daylight saving change, since the page's times follow the game's clock as it was when the page was made; and when the forecast runs out after 14 days.

Places not listed (such as Physis, Ceo Island and Port Cobh) have no weather cycle.

About the "may differ" slots

The game works out a year of weather, one 20-minute slot at a time, by rolling its random number generator with each region's weather table. The generator has a bug: every 625 rolls it reads one number from past the end of its list, and that number is really a spot in the game's memory. So about every 8.7 days, one 20-minute slot is decided by where the game happened to load in memory, not by the weather tables.

Your game decides those slots when it starts and keeps them until you close it, and this page shows exactly what your game will show. But they can be different after you restart the game, for other players, and on the game servers. They fall at the same moment in every region, and often turn out the same way everywhere, all clear or all rain, because they come from the same few numbers.

The servers use their own weather for the few things that check it, like event items that only count in the rain. So during these slots, something that needs rain can fail even though it's raining on your screen, or work when it isn't.

Everything else, over 99.8% of the forecast, is the same for everyone.
