MABINOGI WIKI - READ ME
=======================

The wiki comes in two versions. Most people only need the first one.


1) MabiWiki_Standalone.html  -  the wiki, for you
-------------------------------------------------
- Keep it in your Mabinogi folder (the folder with the "package" folder in it).
  Unzipping the Kanan release into your Mabinogi folder already puts it there.
- Open it from Kanan's menu ("Open Mabinogi Wiki"), type .wiki in chat, or
  double-click it. It opens in your web browser.
- The first time: click "Choose folder", pick the "package" folder inside your
  Mabinogi folder, choose whether you want 3D models, and click "Process".
  Wait until it says it's done.
- After that it opens straight away.
- After a game update, open it and click "Update from your game files".
- Works best in Chrome or Edge.


2) MabiWiki_Hostable  -  only if you want to put the wiki on a website
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Skip this if you just want to use the wiki yourself. You can delete the
folder if you don't need it.

Step 1 - prepare it on your computer FIRST:
  Open the file "index.html" inside the MabiWiki_Hostable folder, then do the
  same as above: choose the "package" folder, pick 3D yes or no, click "Process".
  This fills the folder with the game's data.
  Don't upload the folder before doing this. It won't work.

Step 2 - upload the whole MabiWiki_Hostable folder to a free website host,
  for example:
  - Cloudflare Pages or Netlify: make an account, then drag and drop the
    folder onto their page.
  - GitHub Pages: make a repository, upload the folder's files, then turn on
    "Pages" in the repository's Settings.
  People can then open your website's address. They don't need the game.

With 3D models the folder is big, and some hosts have limits (for example
20,000 files). If it's too big, prepare it again with 3D set to "No".

After a game update: open index.html on your computer again, click
"Update from your game files", then upload the folder again.
